I am an associate professor at the School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. I was an assistant professor from Aug 2020 to Jun 2026. I work on reinforcement learning and game theory. My recent works focus on LLM-RL, learning in games, and information design among multiple agents.

I obtained my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2020, fortunate to be advised by Siu On Chan and Andrej Bogdanov. Before that, I obtained my B.E. in Information Security from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2014, under Hai Zhao.

I am currently a visiting researcher at Vector Institute in Toronto, since Dec 2025, with Yaoliang Yu. I held the same position there during Jan-Aug 2024, and was at University of Waterloo as a visiting assistant professor during May-Sep 2023, with Pascal Poupart.

Research directions

  • Foundations of reinforcement learning. What makes an agent improve reliably over long horizons and from imperfect data, and whether theory can explain the algorithms that train language models.
  • Learning in multi-agent systems. Where many independently learning agents end up when no one coordinates them, and whether that outcome can be predicted rather than only observed.
  • Information and incentive design. How the rules are set rather than played within, determining what agents are told, what they are paid, and what they can credibly promise one another.
  • Ad-hoc topics in learning theory and game theory that I find interesting.
